# Payer B: Member Benefits Policy

- insurer: payer_b
- version: 1

This is the published policy the plan shares with participating hospitals. Each clause is one paragraph so it can be quoted exactly.

## Clause 1: Definitions

In this policy, a covered service is a service described in this policy and billed with a valid HCPCS Level II procedure code and a supporting ICD-10-CM diagnosis code. A claim is a request for payment submitted by a participating hospital on behalf of a member.

## Clause 2: Outpatient and emergency visits

Hospital outpatient clinic visits (G0463), emergency department visits (G0383) and hospital observation services (G0378) are covered when billed with a diagnosis that supports the visit.

## Clause 3: Preventive care

Annual wellness visits (G0438, G0439), depression screening (G0444), screening colonoscopy (G0121), fecal immunochemical testing (G0328), prostate cancer screening (G0103) and breast tomosynthesis (G0279) are covered when billed with the matching diagnosis.

## Clause 4: Prior authorization

Advanced imaging and specialty drugs require prior authorization, and the authorization number must appear on the claim. When a valid prior authorization number is present on the claim, the plan will not deny the claim for missing authorization.

## Clause 5: Referring provider

Hospital outpatient clinic visits (G0463), therapy services (G0283) and durable medical equipment (E0601) require the referring provider's identifier on the claim.

## Clause 6: Advanced imaging

Magnetic resonance imaging and angiography services (C8901, C8908) are covered when prior authorization is on file. Every imaging claim receives an individual review of the submitted clinical information before a coverage decision is made.

## Clause 7: Diabetes self-management training

Diabetes outpatient self-management training (G0108) is covered for members with a diabetes diagnosis, up to the plan's unit maximum per service line.

## Clause 8: Follow-up visits

Follow-up examinations after completed treatment, billed with diagnosis Z09, are covered as outpatient visits.

## Clause 9: Ambulance transport

Emergency ambulance transport (A0429) is covered when the ambulance service line carries an origin and destination modifier.

## Clause 10: Chemotherapy

Chemotherapy administration by infusion (Q0084) includes hydration. Normal saline (J7030) billed on the same claim as Q0084 is included in the administration payment and is not paid separately.

## Clause 11: Timely filing and duplicate claims

Claims must be received within 120 days of the date of service. A claim that repeats a claim the plan has already paid is denied as a duplicate.

## Clause 12: Appeals

Appeals are reviewed against the plan's own claims history. An appeal that alleges a processing error should include comparable claims the plan has paid and describe the denial pattern observed.
