# Payer A: Member Benefits Policy

- insurer: payer_a
- version: 1

This is the published policy the plan shares with participating hospitals. Each clause is one paragraph so it can be quoted exactly.

## Clause 1: Definitions

In this policy, a covered service is a service described in this policy and billed with a valid HCPCS Level II procedure code and a supporting ICD-10-CM diagnosis code. A claim is a request for payment submitted by a participating hospital on behalf of a member.

## Clause 2: Outpatient and emergency visits

Hospital outpatient clinic visits (G0463), emergency department visits (G0383) and hospital observation services (G0378) are covered when billed with a diagnosis that supports the visit.

## Clause 3: Annual wellness visits and preventive screenings

Initial (G0438) and subsequent (G0439) annual wellness visits are covered once per member per year. Services that are part of the initial annual wellness visit, including depression screening (G0444), are included in its payment and are not paid separately on the same claim. Screening colonoscopy (G0121), fecal immunochemical testing (G0328) and prostate cancer screening (G0103) are covered with the matching screening diagnosis.

## Clause 4: Prior authorization

Selected services require prior authorization, including advanced imaging, durable medical equipment, specialty drugs and subsequent annual wellness visits under the plan's care-coordination program. The authorization number must appear on the claim. A claim for such a service without an authorization number is denied as incomplete.

## Clause 5: Referring provider

Durable medical equipment, therapy services and advanced imaging require the referring provider's identifier on the claim. A claim for such a service without it is denied as incomplete.

## Clause 6: Units of service

Drug units and therapy units are paid up to the plan's daily maximum for each service line. Units billed above the maximum on a single line are denied.

## Clause 7: Chronic condition management

Hospital outpatient clinic visits (G0463) for the ongoing management of a chronic condition, including chronic kidney disease (N18.4), type 2 diabetes (E11.9), essential hypertension (I10) and hyperlipidemia (E78.5), are covered and are not subject to medical-necessity review.

## Clause 8: Ambulance transport

Emergency ambulance transport (A0429) is covered. The ambulance service line must carry an origin and destination modifier, for example RH for residence to hospital.

## Clause 9: Same-day services

When a problem-oriented clinic visit (G0463) is billed on the same claim as an annual wellness visit, modifier 25 must be appended to the clinic visit line.

## Clause 10: Specialty drugs and chemotherapy

Trastuzumab (J9355) and chemotherapy administration by infusion (Q0084) are covered for a documented cancer diagnosis when prior authorization is on file.

## Clause 11: Timely filing and duplicate claims

Claims must be received within 90 days of the date of service; late claims are denied. A claim that repeats a claim the plan has already paid is denied as a duplicate.

## Clause 12: Appeals

A hospital may appeal a denial within 180 days. The appeal should identify, by clause number, the clause of this policy that supports coverage.
