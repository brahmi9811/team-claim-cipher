# Payer C: Member Benefits Policy

- insurer: payer_c
- version: 1

This is the published policy the plan shares with participating hospitals. Each clause is one paragraph so it can be quoted exactly.

## Clause 1: Definitions

In this policy, a covered service is a service described in this policy and billed with a valid HCPCS Level II procedure code and a supporting ICD-10-CM diagnosis code. A claim is a request for payment submitted by a participating hospital on behalf of a member.

## Clause 2: Outpatient visits and observation

Hospital outpatient clinic visits (G0463) and hospital observation services (G0378) are covered when billed with a diagnosis that supports the visit.

## Clause 3: Annual wellness visits

Initial (G0438) and subsequent (G0439) annual wellness visits are covered once per member per year. Depression screening (G0444) is part of the initial annual wellness visit and is not paid separately on the same claim. A problem-oriented clinic visit billed with an annual wellness visit needs modifier 25.

## Clause 4: Prior authorization

Specialty drugs, durable medical equipment and subsequent annual wellness visits require prior authorization, and the authorization number must appear on the claim.

## Clause 5: Visits for acute symptoms

Emergency department visits (G0383) and clinic visits (G0463) for acute symptoms such as chest pain (R07.9) or abdominal pain (R10.9) are covered when a prudent layperson would reasonably believe the symptoms needed prompt care. Coverage does not depend on the final diagnosis.

## Clause 6: Units of service

Drug units and observation hours are paid up to the plan's maximum for each service line. Units above the maximum on a single line are denied.

## Clause 7: Referring provider

Advanced imaging requires the referring provider's identifier on the claim.

## Clause 8: Preventive screenings

Screening colonoscopy (G0121), fecal immunochemical testing (G0328), prostate cancer screening (G0103) and breast tomosynthesis (G0279) are covered when billed with the matching diagnosis.

## Clause 9: Ambulance transport

Emergency ambulance transport (A0429) is covered.

## Clause 10: Specialty drugs and chemotherapy

Trastuzumab (J9355) and chemotherapy administration by infusion (Q0084) are covered for a documented cancer diagnosis.

## Clause 11: Timely filing and duplicate claims

Claims must be received within 120 days of the date of service. A claim that repeats a claim the plan has already paid is denied as a duplicate.

## Clause 12: Appeals

Appeals must reference the current version of this policy. The plan does not accept citations of superseded versions.
